    HVAC Engineering

    Complete HVAC consultancy from load calculations, ventilation and air conditioning design to duct layout, equipment selection, and commissioning. Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning system design Load calculations, psychrometric analysis and energy optimization Ducting, piping, and equipment layout coordination Ventilation, pressurization and air quality strategy Testing, balancing and commissioning support

    Electrical Engineering

    Comprehensive electrical system design for power distribution, lighting, grounding, emergency power and renewable integration. HT/LT power distribution system design Lighting, power, earthing and lightning protection DG set, UPS, capacitor and solar power design Substation, transformer and switchgear layouts Testing, commissioning and project coordination

    Electrical Low Voltage Systems

    Low-voltage consultancy for security, communication, automation and structured cabling to support modern building control and connectivity. CCTV, access control and public address systems Data, voice, TV and structured cabling infrastructure Home automation and smart building system design FTTH and network distribution planning System integration and commissioning support

    Public Health Engineering

    Comprehensive plumbing and public health design for water supply, drainage, rainwater harvesting and waste handling across buildings. Domestic and industrial water supply systems Hot water generation and water treatment design Rainwater harvesting and reuse planning Sewage, septic and sewerage system coordination

    Fire Protection Systems

    Fire protection consultancy covering hydrant, sprinkler, suppression, alarm and evacuation systems for safe buildings. Hydrant, sprinkler and water-based fire protection design Clean agent and gas suppression system planning Fire alarm, detection and public address systems Pump sizing, hydraulic calculations and hydraulic layouts Fire safety coordination, approval and commissioning support

    BIM & Green Building Advisory

    Advisory support for green building rating, BIM coordination, building management systems, and fire-life safety planning. Green building certification advisory BIM coordination and drawing management Building management and automation support Vertical transportation and fire-life safety coordination Peer review, planning approval and constructability reviews
