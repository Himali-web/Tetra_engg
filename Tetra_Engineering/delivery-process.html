    PROJECT JOURNEY

    Design Stages

    The Scope of MEP Services covers all essential elements of a project. Tetra Engineering Consultants are involved from Space Planning through completion, providing valuable technical inputs at every stage of the journey.

    01
    Stage 01

    Conceptual Design

    We turn the brief and architectural intent into a clear MEP strategy — defining systems, loads and space requirements that anchor the entire project.

    Key deliverables
    • Space Planning
    • Issuing standard list of Drawings
    • Filling Query List
    • Issuing Design Basis Report
    • Issuing Equipment Selection Sheet
    02
    Stage 02

    Schematic Design

    Schemes are developed, co-ordinated and budgeted so every discipline is aligned before detailed design begins.

    Key deliverables
    • Issuing schematic drawings
    • Finalization of schemes
    • Co-ordination with Architects / Structure / Landscape etc. Consultants
    • Preparing budgetary estimates
    03
    Stage 03

    Detail Design

    Agreed schematics evolve into fully co-ordinated, construction-ready layouts for every service.

    Key deliverables
    • Issuing detailed design based on agreed schematics
    • Issuing builder's work layout for all services
    • Issuing equipment room layouts
    04
    Stage 04

    Tender

    Complete, cross-checked tender packages let contractors bid with confidence and full clarity.

    Key deliverables
    • Issuing General Conditions
    • Issuing Special Conditions
    • Cross checking overall design parameters, equipment and costing
    • Issuing tender document & drawings
    05
    Stage 05

    GFC Drawings & Approval

    Good-for-construction drawings are issued and contractor submittals are reviewed to keep works compliant and on track.

    Key deliverables
    • Issuing GFC drawings
    • Approval of shop drawings prepared by contractors
    • Approval of technical submittals
    • Assessing and certifying bills of contractors
    06
    Stage 06

    Project Completion & Handover

    Systems are commissioned and documentation is handed over so the building performs exactly as designed from day one.

    Key deliverables
    • Issuing as-built drawings
    • System testing & commissioning
    • Submitting operation & maintenance manuals
    • Conducting staff training & system handover
    • Supporting final close-out & project certification
