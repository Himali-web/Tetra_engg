DELIVERY PROCESS

The Scope of MEP Services shall cover all essential elements of a Project. Tetra Engineering Consultants shall be involved in the entire project giving its valuable inputs right from the Space Planning up to completion of project.

Stage I – Conceptual Design
▪ Space Planning.
▪ Issuing standard list of Drawings.
▪ Filling Query List.
▪ Issuing Design Basis Report.
▪ Issuing Equipment Selection Sheet.

Stage II – Schematic Design
▪ Issuing schematic drawings.
▪ Finalization of schemes.
▪ Co-ordination with Architects / Structure / Landscape etc. Consultants.
▪ Preparing budgetary estimates.

Stage III – Detail Design
▪ Issuing detailed design based on agreed schematics.
▪ Issuing builder's work layout for all services.
▪ Issuing equipment room layouts.

Stage IV - Tender
▪ Issuing General Conditions
▪ Issuing Special Conditions
▪ Cross checking overall design parameters, equipment and costing.
▪ Issuing tender document & drawings

Stage V – GFC drawings & Approval
▪ Issuing GFC drawings.
▪ Approval of shop drawings prepared by contractors.
▪ Approval of technical submittals
▪ Assessing and certifying bills of contractors.

D. PUBLIC HEALTH ENGINEERING

I. Water Supply
▪ Study and analyze basic data with respect to water requirement, consumption and storage including fire-fighting needs with due consideration to existing / future plans.
▪ Design transmission line from specified water source to storage facilities.
▪ Based on water quality, determine need of water treatment.
▪ Determine underground / overhead storage facilities.
▪ Design water distribution pipelines from storage tanks to various water consumption centers and / or Hydro pneumatic system.
▪ Design hot water distribution system for solar hot water system, if any.
▪ Prepare detailed drawings for laying transmission and distribution pipelines.
▪ Prepare detailed specifications for water supply components / piping.

II. Drainage/Sanitation
▪ Study existing contour plan and decide tentative pipeline alignment for sewage. Study alignment survey details at site and prepare relevant drawings for laying sewer.
▪ Design sewer lines based on data and above contour/alignment survey.
▪ Prepare detailed specifications for piping system.
▪ Prepare sections for total piping system.
▪ Prepare drawings for other work like manhole, flushing tank and bathroom/toilet/drawings etc.
▪ Design septic tank, soak pits. Capacity of sewage treatment plant and its plant space will be provided along with water balance diagram showing disposal/reuse of treated waste water will be provided.

III. Storm water / Rain Water Harvesting
▪ Collect data with respect to total rainfall, rainfall intensity, contributing area etc.
▪ Study existing contour plan and determine economical alignment for storm water drainage system.
▪ Design storm water including roof drainage / area drainage lines.
▪ To prepare drawing of identified alignments for execution purpose.
▪ To prepare drawings for appurtenances like inlet drain, chambers/man holes etc.
▪ Design / prepare storm water harvesting tank for roof top as well as area drainage.

E. FIRE PROTECTION SYSTEM
▪ Classification of occupancies as per governing guidelines.
▪ Design the Storage Requirement for Fire-Fighting systems like, Hydrant, Sprinkler & water curtain system.
▪ Design detailed GA Layout for all equipment & pumps for Fire-Fighting.
▪ To design pump requirements and provide pump specifications.
▪ Design of Fire Fighting Sprinkler system as per the governing guidelines & standards.
▪ Design Firefighting Hydrant system as per the governing guidelines & standards.
▪ Design water curtain/drencher/façade cooling system as per the guidelines & standards.
▪ Design a Fire Alarm System required for building.
▪ Design optimum fire system including sprinkler system, compartmentalization, wet riser & schematic system.
▪ Prepare operation logic & sequence of operation for all the firefighting systems.
